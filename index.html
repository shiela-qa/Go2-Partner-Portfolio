This guide explains how partners are classified, scored, and tracked in the Partner Portfolio Tracker. Use it to ensure consistent data entry across the team.
Partner Status
Healthy
Active & stable
Partner is engaged, staffing is stable, no outstanding concerns. Default status for all active partners unless flagged otherwise.
At Risk
Needs attention
Partner has shown signals of potential churn — reduced seats, missed check-ins, payment issues, or expressed dissatisfaction. Partners with high seat counts (Tier 1) and partners with 3 or more EM transitions are also considered At Risk by default due to elevated revenue exposure and account instability. Check the Last Note field for context on why this status was assigned. Retention Approach and Risk Flags should be filled in for all At Risk accounts.
Churned
No longer active
Partner has ended their engagement. Record is kept for historical reference. Churn Date and Months Active should be filled in before closing.
Revenue Tier
Tier 1
5+ teammates
Highest-value partners. Large seat counts mean higher revenue and higher risk if churned. Require the most active EM engagement and proactive health monitoring.
Tier 2
2–4 teammates
Mid-size partners. Growing accounts can move to Tier 1. Regular check-ins and expansion opportunities should be tracked.
Tier 3
1 teammate
Smallest accounts. High in volume, lower in individual risk. Monitor for growth signals. Can be upgraded if seats expand.
Priority
Standard
Default priority
No special escalation needed. Normal cadence of check-ins and reviews applies.
High
Escalated focus
Partner requires elevated attention. Applies to At Risk partners, all Tier 1 (high seat count) accounts, partners with 3 or more EM transitions, and partners mid-transition. EM should check in more frequently than the standard cadence.
Closed
Churned / archived
Assigned automatically when a partner's status is set to Churned. No further EM action required.
Partner Record — Field Definitions
FieldTypeDefinition & guidance
Partner NameTextLegal or commonly used business name. For multi-account partners (e.g. BroadVoice Support, BroadVoice Support (1)) append a parenthetical to distinguish.
StatusSelectHealthy · At Risk · Churned. Updated by the assigned EM at each review cycle.
Current EMSelectThe Engagement Manager currently responsible for this partner. Must match a name in the EM roster exactly.
EM TransitionsNumberCount of how many times this partner has changed EMs. High numbers (3+) may indicate instability. Populated from the Transition Log.
Partner SinceYYYY-MMMonth the partner first became active. Used to calculate Months Active.
Churn DateYYYY-MMMonth the partner churned. Leave blank for active partners.
Months ActiveCalculatedDifference between Partner Since and Churn Date (or today for active). Populated automatically in the source sheet.
TeammatesNumberCurrent active headcount staffed to this partner. Used to determine Revenue Tier and Seat totals.
Revenue TierSelectTier 1 (5+ seats) · Tier 2 (2–4 seats) · Tier 3 (1 TM). Should be kept in sync with Teammates count.
Risk FlagsTextFree-text notes on specific risks — e.g. "payment delays", "key contact left", "competitor evaluation". Separate multiple flags with semicolons.
Retention ApproachTextActive strategy being used — e.g. "backup TM placed", "discount applied", "escalated to QA". Required when Status = At Risk.
Last NoteTextMost recent EM observation or action. Keep concise (1–2 sentences). Include date prefix for context: "May 2026 — new POC reKO done".
ActiveYes / NoYes = partner is currently active. No = churned or inactive. Drives the Active Partners view and all KPI counts.
PrioritySelectStandard · High · Closed. Set by EM or leadership to flag accounts needing elevated attention.
Update Cadence & Workflow
FrequencyWhat to update
Every 2 weeksExport CSVs from the source Google Sheet and share with Shiela to regenerate the dashboard. Confirm Teammates count is accurate.
MonthlyReview Status for all assigned partners. Update Last Note. Flag any At Risk accounts with a Retention Approach.
When it happensLog EM transitions in the Transition Log CSV immediately — include From EM, To EM, Date, and Reason. Update Current EM in Partners tab.
When it happensMark partner as Churned, fill Churn Date, and set Active = No. Move Priority to Closed.
QuarterlyReview all At Risk partners — ensure Risk Flags and Retention Approach are filled in. Review EM workload distribution. Update Revenue Tiers if headcount has changed.
